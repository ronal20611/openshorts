This Privacy Policy explains how OpenShorts (the “Application”) handles information when you use it. Please read it before connecting any third-party account.

Depending on the features you use, information may include video files and other content you select, captions and publication details you enter, and account or service information needed to connect to a third-party platform.

2. Third-party account connections

If you connect TikTok or another supported service, the Application may use the authorization process and permissions provided by that service. The information made available can depend on the permissions you approve and the service’s API. The Application uses that access to provide the feature you request, such as preparing or submitting content for publication.

Third-party platforms process information under their own privacy policies. Review those policies before connecting an account. You can manage or revoke an application's access through the relevant platform when that platform provides this option.

3. Where information is processed

OpenShorts is designed as a desktop application. Information you select or enter may be processed on the device where you run the Application. When you use a publishing or account-connection feature, relevant information may also be transmitted to the third-party service you choose. This policy does not claim that every feature or version handles information in exactly the same way; consult the documentation for the version you use.

4. How information is used

Information is used to operate the features you request, connect to authorized third-party services, prepare or submit content, troubleshoot issues, and maintain the security and reliability of the Application. Do not submit information that is not needed for the feature you are using.

5. Sharing

OpenShorts does not sell your personal information. Information is shared with a third-party platform when you choose to use a feature that requires that platform, and with any other service only when the feature you choose requires it. The Application's actual network behavior may vary by version and configuration.

6. Storage and security

Information handled by the Application may be stored on your device or by the third-party services you connect to. You are responsible for securing your device, operating-system account, backups, and any locally stored credentials or tokens. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

7. Retention and deletion

Content and settings stored locally can generally be managed or deleted using the Application or your device's file-management tools, depending on the version. Information held by a third-party platform must be managed through that platform. Removing the Application may not automatically delete information already held by third parties.

8. Children's privacy

The Application is not directed to children under the age required by applicable law to use the relevant services. Do not use the Application if you are not legally permitted to do so.
